As of July 17, 2026, New Jersey employers must provide job-protected leave to more workers. The law has expanded in two key ways:

Workers receiving Temporary Disability Insurance or Family Leave Insurance benefits now have job protection during their leave if not already covered by the New Jersey Family Leave Act or federal Family and Medical Leave Act. There's no minimum employer size for this requirement. A worker’s eligibility for benefits is based on their recent [earnings](https://nj.gov/labor/myleavebenefits/worker/tdi#earnings).

The New Jersey Family Leave Act now covers employers with 15 or more employees worldwide (down from 30), in addition to continuing to cover state and local government agencies of all sizes. Employees qualify after three months and 250 hours worked (down from 12 months and 1,000 hours). These workers remain eligible for up to 12 weeks of protected leave in a 24-month period.

Review the updated guidance to ensure you're in compliance.
