New Jersey businesses managing environmental compliance can attend the 25th Annual Multimedia Regulatory Update Conference on November 20, 2026, from 9:00 AM to 4:15 PM. The virtual conference by the New Jersey Department of Environmental Protection covers regulatory updates affecting air quality, water quality, waste management, contaminated site remediation, climate change, environmental justice, and enforcement.

The conference is designed for businesses and professionals who need to stay current on NJDEP regulatory initiatives. Topics include emerging contaminants, materials sustainability, energy regulations, and compliance requirements across multiple environmental programs. The [full agenda](https://dep.nj.gov/wp-content/uploads/srp/25th_annual_njdep_awma_reg_agenda.pdf) (PDF) is available.
