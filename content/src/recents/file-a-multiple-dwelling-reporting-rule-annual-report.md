If you own an apartment complex with 25 units or more in New Jersey, you must file an annual report with the New Jersey Division on Civil Rights (DCR).

The report is called the Multiple Dwelling Reporting Rule Report (MDRR Report) and requires you to:

* Collect demographic information about your apartment applicants and tenants (see below for details)
* File the information with DCR by January 30 each year
* Keep the information for 3 years, separate from your rental records

You must file an annual report for each apartment complex you own. 

### MDRR Annual Report Requirements

Download and review the MDRR Frequently Asked Questions document (PDF) before you complete and file your annual report.

The information on the annual report helps DCR study patterns of housing occupancy and enforce the New Jersey Law Against Discrimination. You need to submit information such as:

* Unit breakdown (number of units, rental rates, and utilities included)
* Race and ethnicity of your tenants and apartment applicants
* Number of apartment rental turnovers
* Number of Section 8 units
* Advertising and recruiting techniques
* Whether the apartment is subsidized by the Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) or another government agency
* Whether the apartment is accessible to people with disabilities

Use the MDRR Tenant/Applicant Inquiry Form to collect and record race and ethnicity data for the annual report only. 

### How to File the MDRR Annual Report

File the annual report online. If you are creating a new account, you must enter a 4-digit PIN, which you can find in a notice DCR sends via email or standard mail each December. If you did not receive a notice, contact DCR’s MDRR unit at (609) 376-3295 or email DCRMDRR@NJCivilRights.gov.

**Once completed, you will have:**

A filed annual report.

**What happens if I don’t file a report?**

If you don’t file your report online by the deadline, you’ll have to pay a late filing or penalty fee.

**Agency:** NJ Division of Civil Rights

**Contact:** (609) 376-3295 or DCRMDRR@NJCivilRights.gov
