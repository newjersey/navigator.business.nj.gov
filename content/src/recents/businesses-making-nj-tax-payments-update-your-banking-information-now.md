New Jersey changed its ACH banking routing and account numbers on May 27, 2026. If you make automated tax or employer payments to the state and haven't updated your payment software, your payments are failing now.

The deadline to update was June 30. Payments submitted with old banking information after that date will not go through. Failed payments are your responsibility to correct and may subject you to penalties and interest.

Contact your financial institution or software provider immediately to update the state's routing and account numbers for all New Jersey ACH credit tax and employer payments.
