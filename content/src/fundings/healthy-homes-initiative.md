## Eligibility

* Units must be part of a development
* The development must get funding, or be applying for funding, from 1 of these DCA Housing Production programs: the New Jersey Affordable Housing Trust Fund (Balanced Housing), HOME Housing Production Investment (HPI), HOME CHDO, HOME ARP, or the National Housing Trust Fund. DMAHS and NJDCA can also send Healthy Homes funding to other Federal, State, County, or Local programs
* Healthy Homes units cannot make up more than 50% of the total units in the development. The total project size cannot go over 10 units. DCA and Medicaid may approve larger projects with more than 10 units. They will review these projects case-by-case
* Units must follow the federal Home and Community-Based Services (HCBS) Settings Rule. This means units must be part of the surrounding community. They cannot be located with inpatient institutional treatment or on the grounds of a public institution that provides Medicaid services
* Units on the first floor get priority
* Units have to be adaptable. Accessible units will get priority.

:::largeCallout{ showHeader="true" headerText="Benefits" calloutType="conditional" amountIconText="" filingTypeIconText="" frequencyIconText="" phoneIconText="" phoneIconLabel="" emailIconText="" }

The program offers two types of funding:

1. **Capital funding:** A 30-year loan with 0% interest. DCA forgives the loan if the project meets all requirements
2. **Repair and Replacement (R&R) reserve:** A savings account that pays for repairs over 30 years

This funding lowers the developer's costs. It also covers utilities, management fees, taxes, and insurance. Tenants don't pay security deposits because the R&R reserve covers normal wear and tear.

:::
