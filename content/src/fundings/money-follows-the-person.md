## Eligibility

* The project is located in 1 of these counties: Bergen, Passaic, Essex, Morris, Hudson, Monmouth, or Ocean
* The project has up to 5 units for people moving from institutional care into community living
* The project meets the Money Follows the Person Program guideline requirements
* The project meets requirements for HMFA Multifamily Underwriting Guidelines and Financing Policy 



:::largeCallout{ showHeader="true" headerText="Benefits" calloutType="conditional" amountIconText="" filingTypeIconText="" frequencyIconText="" phoneIconText="" phoneIconLabel="" emailIconText="" }

Loans up to $150,000 total for up to 5 units that serve people transitioning from institutions. Loans are structured as cash flow loans repaid from project income.

:::
