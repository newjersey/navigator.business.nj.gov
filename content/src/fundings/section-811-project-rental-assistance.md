## Eligibility

* The project sets aside up to 25% of its units for people with disabilities
* The project meets all HMFA rules for underwriting and financing policy
* The project combines rental assistance with 4% or 9% Low-Income Housing Tax Credits, and uses NJHMFA Agency Financing
* The project has more than 5 units
* The project is not 100% project-based assisted

:::largeCallout{ showHeader="true" headerText="Benefits" calloutType="conditional" amountIconText="" filingTypeIconText="" frequencyIconText="" phoneIconText="" phoneIconLabel="" emailIconText="" }

Rental subsidies cover the gap between what tenants can afford and the actual rent. Tenants typically pay 30% of their income toward rent. The subsidy is paid directly to the property owner on behalf of eligible tenants. Up to 25% of units in a project can get Section 811 rental assistance.

:::
