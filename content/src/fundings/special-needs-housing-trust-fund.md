## Eligibility

* The project includes units that serve people with special needs
* The project can meet requirements for the Special Needs Housing Trust Fund Program, HMFA Multifamily Underwriting Guidelines and Financing Policy requirements (if applicable)   

:::largeCallout{ showHeader="true" headerText="Benefits" calloutType="conditional" amountIconText="" filingTypeIconText="" frequencyIconText="" phoneIconText="" phoneIconLabel="" emailIconText="" }

The Trust Fund will not finance more than 80% of the total development cost of any project. Projects receive loans up to $4 million. For projects with both special needs housing and general affordable housing, loans are capped at $150,000 per special needs unit. All loans are repaid from the project's rental income over time.

:::
