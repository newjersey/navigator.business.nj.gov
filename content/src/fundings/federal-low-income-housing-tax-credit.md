## Eligibility

* The project builds new affordable rental housing, or repairs and preserves existing affordable rental housing
* The project serves low- and moderate-income households (50% and 60% of Area Median Income) and working families (80% of Area Median Income)
* The project agrees to a 30-year or 45-year deed restriction to keep units affordable long-term
* First-time developers should have experience, or partner with an experienced developer or consultant

:::largeCallout{ showHeader="true" headerText="Benefits" calloutType="conditional" amountIconText="" filingTypeIconText="" frequencyIconText="" phoneIconText="" phoneIconLabel="" emailIconText="" }

The tax credit gives investors a dollar-for-dollar reduction in their federal taxes. This makes it easier for developers to attract investor funding. Award amounts vary by credit type:

* Projects in the 9% LIHTC Family Cycle can receive up to $2,000,000 per year
* Projects in the 9% LIHTC Age-Friendly Senior Cycle or Supportive Housing Cycle can receive up to $1,600,000 per year
* Projects using 4% LIHTC are capped by the project's eligible basis limit, which can change based on market conditions

:::
