## Eligibility

Organizations must meet all of these requirements:

* Be a nonprofit developer incorporated and in good standing in New Jersey
* Have an established partnership with one of these 10 NJHOMES Launchpad municipalities: Far Hills, Palisades Park, Salem, East Orange, Montclair Township, Mendham Township, Fairfield Township, Stafford Township, East Amwell Township, or Evesham Township

Projects must meet all of these requirements:

* Be 100% affordable housing
* Have 25 units or fewer
* Have an identified project site before applying
* Submit only 1 application per organization per State Fiscal Year (July 1 through June 30)

:::largeCallout{ showHeader="true" headerText="Benefits" calloutType="conditional" amountIconText="" filingTypeIconText="" frequencyIconText="" phoneIconText="" phoneIconLabel="" emailIconText="" }

The program offers two loan types: Site Control Loans and Seed Money Loans. Each offers up to $75,000, with a combined maximum of $150,000 per development.

* **Site Control Loans** cover pre-site-control costs, like feasibility studies, legal fees, and acquisition.
* **Seed Money Loans** cover pre-construction costs after site control, like architectural, engineering, and zoning approval fees.

Seed Money Loans have site control, financing, staff, and market requirements. Both loans carry 0% interest and pay out 100% upfront. DCA may forgive a loan if barriers arise beyond the borrower's control.

:::
