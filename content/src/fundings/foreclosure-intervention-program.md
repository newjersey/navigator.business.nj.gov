## Eligibility

* The developer is a community development financial institution or other nonprofit organization
* The developer has experience in home rehabilitation, mortgage servicing and underwriting, real estate financing and acquisition for affordable housing, or community development work
* The developer has strong knowledge of the New Jersey real estate market
* The developer finds eligible properties and gets project approval from NJHMFA
* The developer buys and repairs the properties, hires third-party specialists as needed, and gets certificates of occupancy
* The developer sells the properties to households earning at or below 120% of Area Median Income
* By law, the developer must give preference to buyers who hold an NJHMFA-issued mortgage

:::largeCallout{ showHeader="true" headerText="Benefits" calloutType="conditional" amountIconText="" filingTypeIconText="" frequencyIconText="" phoneIconText="" phoneIconLabel="" emailIconText="" }

FIP provides a grant of up to $400,000 per eligible property. This funding covers buying and repairing vacant or abandoned homes.

:::
