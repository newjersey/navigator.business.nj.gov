## Eligibility

Organizations must follow these requirements:

* Be a nonprofit or for-profit developer in New Jersey

All projects must follow these requirements:

* Build new rental housing or repair existing rental housing
* Serve families who earn 30% or less of the area median income 
* Serve people with special needs, and identify which special needs populations you will serve when you apply
* Have experience serving people with special needs
* Complete the project within 4 years, including renting all units
* Receive a Section 8 Project Based Voucher for each unit funded by NHTF (maximum 4 vouchers per project)

:::largeCallout{ showHeader="true" headerText="Benefits" calloutType="conditional" amountIconText="" filingTypeIconText="" frequencyIconText="" phoneIconText="" phoneIconLabel="" emailIconText="" }

Loans up to $1 million per project. Rental units must stay affordable for 30 years, with rent limits based on unit size and location. NHTF is not your only funding source—you must combine it with other funding.

:::
